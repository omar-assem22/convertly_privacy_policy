Privacy Policy for Convertly

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Convertly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application, Convertly.

1. Information We Collect

Convertly does not collect or store any personal data directly on external servers. All your conversion history, preferences, and custom presets are stored locally on your device using local storage.

2. Third-Party Services & Ad Data

To keep Convertly free, we display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, primarily Google AdMob.

These third-party providers may automatically collect certain non-personal device information to serve relevant ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. This information may include:

  • Device Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • IP address (used to infer broad location like city/country)
  • App performance metrics and ad interaction data

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Local Storage

Convertly uses local device storage to remember your app settings, selected unit preferences, and custom conversion presets. This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to us or third parties.

4. Children’s Privacy

Convertly does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action to remove it.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
